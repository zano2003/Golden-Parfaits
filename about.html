About Golden Parfaits

Golden Parfaits is a student-owned business created to provide delicious and enjoyable treats at affordable, student-friendly prices. We offer a selection of sweet treats and beverages designed for students, university employees, and people within the surrounding university environment. Our products include cake parfaits, ice cream, ice lollies, cake slices, and a selection of hot and cold beverages. We aim to combine quality, affordability, and convenience so that our customers can enjoy something special without having to spend too much.

Our Story

Golden Parfaits was created by two students who recognised the opportunity to provide enjoyable treats within the university environment. We wanted to create a business that would offer products that feel luxurious and special while remaining affordable for our customers. As students ourselves, we understand the importance of affordability and convenience. This inspired us to create a business that makes it easier for people within the university environment to enjoy a delicious treat without breaking the bank.

Our Mission and Values

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide delicious, affordable, and enjoyable treats while making the ordering process convenient for our customers.

Our Values

  • Affordability – Keeping our products accessible to students and university employees.
  • Quality – Providing treats that customers can enjoy and appreciate.
  • Convenience – Making it easy for customers to explore, order, and receive their products.
  • Customer Satisfaction – Creating a positive experience from browsing the website to enjoying the final product.

What Makes Us Different?

Golden Parfaits focuses on bringing together the feeling of luxury with affordable prices. Our goal is to make everyday treats feel special without making them unnecessarily expensive. We also focus on convenience by providing delivery and simple ordering through WhatsApp. Customers can browse our products, compare prices, choose what they would like, and easily proceed to place an order.
